## Summary

The support loop worked as a sales aid: the AI made a necessary operational request, remained the owner, resumed naturally, applied the answer to the buyer's budget/view trade-off, and progressed the buyer to appointment readiness before an operational handoff. The synthetic support answer is simulator-only evidence, not Pearlmont factual Knowledge or evidence of a live inventory connection. The AI materially overstated that evidence by claiming the unit was available. The buyer was not booked: the human could not verify availability or a slot. Direct answer to the primary question: when support returns a useful verified answer, this AI can use it to continue selling and move a suitable buyer toward Appointment Ready, but this run exposes a serious reliability gap in distinguishing a fixture-backed unit result from verified current availability.
