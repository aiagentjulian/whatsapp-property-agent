## Conversation summary

The agent was cautious, relevant, and honest about the family's space concern and the need to verify affordability. The buyer showed conditional viewing interest, but no appointment was confirmed. The main weakness was repetitive maintenance instead of setting a simple follow-up after the requested documents are ready.

## What the Agent did well

- Responded directly to the initial price question and avoided presenting an unverified effective price as final.
- Asked for a relevant loan-tenure assumption and passed the buyer's budget target to the colleague checking affordability.
- Addressed the household's size objection with practical layout information and a candid caveat.
- Did not pressure the buyer to book before the spouse had reviewed the information.
