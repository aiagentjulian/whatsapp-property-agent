# Independent sales judging

Score each dimension 0–10, cite actual customer/sales turn text for every score.
Weighted total = discovery × 2 + connection × 3.5 + progression × 3.5 + natural × 1.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence to assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Effective Discovery | 20% | A useful buying motive, not quantity of CRM fields |
| Product Connection & Buying Desire | 35% | Verified property advantage connects to expressed need; buyer's reaction |
| Sales Progression / Appointment Opportunity | 35% | Timely relevant next step; low-pressure viewing when appropriate, no forced invite |
| Natural Conversation | 10% | Responsive, concise, human; no repetitive questioning or brochure dump |

Separately mark factual accuracy and handoff compliance PASS / FAIL / UNCERTAIN,
with specific claims and Knowledge source evidence. Planned features stay planned;
no invented rent, yield, appreciation, furniture fit or confirmed appointment.
Invitation ≠ customer agreement ≠ human handoff ≠ confirmed booking.

Do not require budget/timeline/household completion before viewing. Do not reward
random selling points, endless dialogue, mentioning viewing alone, or agent's
self-described strategy. Treat actual customer interest and reaction as evidence.
Use the complete short segment, acknowledge ambiguity and avoid inflated precision.

Predeclared selection: candidate must improve mean by at least 5/100, with no new
severe fact/handoff issue and no development case declining by over 10 points.
Holdout must show a useful response/progression and no serious boundary failure.
A marginal or conflicting result is NEEDS REVIEW, not proof of improved selling.
