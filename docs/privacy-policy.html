Privacy Policy

WhatsApp Property Agent test app · Last updated 8 October 2026

This is a privately operated test app for handling property enquiries sent to its WhatsApp test number. It is intended for controlled testing with approved contacts, not as a general-purpose or production customer service.

Information the app handles

When you message the test number, the app receives your WhatsApp phone number and message. It may also keep the resulting conversation and property-enquiry details, such as your name, preferred area or property type, budget range, financing context, purchase timeline, questions, concerns, sales stage, and next action. If you ask for human help, the related support or handoff details may also be recorded.

How information is used

The app uses inbound text messages to maintain a lead record and generate a property-sales reply. Replies are generated by an AI Agent and may be incomplete or inaccurate. The app can also record support requests or hand a conversation to a human operator. It does not use this inbound workflow to initiate unsolicited marketing messages.

Services that process information

  • Meta / WhatsApp: delivers messages and message-status events to the app and carries replies back to WhatsApp.
  • OpenAI: receives the message and relevant conversation and lead context to generate the AI reply. The app requests the OpenAI Responses API with response storage disabled (store: false). OpenAI processes API requests under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Google Sheets: receives lead and CRM fields so the operator can review and manage enquiries.

Storage and retention

The app keeps conversation messages, lead profiles, support and handoff records, and AI usage metadata in a local SQLite database on the operator’s computer. Lead records are also synchronized to a Google Sheets CRM. The app has no automatic deletion schedule; the operator must delete records from the local database and CRM when they are no longer needed. Meta, OpenAI, and Google may handle data under their own retention and privacy terms.

Access and disclosure

The operator can access the local records and CRM to run the test and respond to enquiries. Information is sent to the services listed above only as needed to deliver WhatsApp messaging, generate replies, and maintain the CRM. The app does not sell enquiry information.

Your choices and requests

You can stop participating by not sending further messages. To ask a question, correct information, or request deletion of your local and CRM records, contact the app operator through the same WhatsApp conversation. The operator can review and remove records manually; copies held by Meta, OpenAI, or Google are subject to those providers’ processes.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the test app’s data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

This policy applies to the WhatsApp Property Agent test app operated with the public project repository.
