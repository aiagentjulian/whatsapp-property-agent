# Pearlmont — Booking & Payment Process (Internal)

## Purpose

Internal operational knowledge for booking/payment handling.

This is not general customer-facing FAQ material.

The Agent should normally move toward human handoff once the customer is at the stage of actual booking, payment execution, package confirmation, or unit reservation.

---

## Booking Fee Refund Rule

Latest FAQ states:

- booking fee is refundable with **2 loan rejection letters**
- if purchaser cancels without loan rejection, **RM300 may be forfeited**

Do not simplify this into "booking is fully refundable" without the stated condition.

---

## Loan Margin Context

Latest FAQ lists panel-financier examples that may support higher financing margins:

- 95%: PBB, HLB, RHB (Green RE), MBB (Green RE)
- 100%: PBB, Bank Islam, and selected SJKP / bank arrangements referenced in the FAQ

These are project-source references, not approval guarantees.

Actual eligibility depends on purchaser profile, bank assessment, current bank policy, and applicable scheme terms.

---

## 100% Financing / First Disbursement

Latest FAQ states that for a purchaser applying for 100% financing, the first 10% disbursement to the developer is subject to confirmation and solicitor advice.

Do not give a fixed disbursement timeline beyond this source.

---

## Progressive Payment

Latest FAQ states the approved progressive payment schedule follows KPKT approval.

If the customer requires exact legal/payment milestones, use the SPA / solicitor-confirmed schedule rather than paraphrasing from memory.

---

## Developer Payment QR

A DuitNow QR has been supplied for **SkyWorld Mutiara Development** as the developer payment account reference.

The QR image itself is operational payment material.

Rules:

- do not send it casually during early sales conversation
- do not use it as a generic project brochure asset
- do not ask a customer to pay simply because they show interest
- use only once unit / package / booking details have been confirmed through the proper sales process
- payment-stage communication should normally be under HUMAN ownership or explicit human authorization
- when sending a payment QR, the customer should be told what the payment is for and the amount to be paid
- do not alter, recreate, or substitute the QR from memory

---

## Handoff Point

The AI Sales Agent's primary conversion objective is to secure a qualified viewing / appointment.

Actual booking, payment, discount negotiation, unit reservation, SPA coordination, and final financing handling normally belong to the human sales process.

Therefore, a customer who reaches payment-ready / booking-ready status should generally be handed off rather than kept in autonomous AI closing.

---

## Safety Rule

Never fabricate:

- payment amount
- booking amount
- bank / merchant account details
- payment confirmation
- receipt status
- unit reservation status
- final package

Any mismatch or uncertainty should be confirmed by a human before payment proceeds.
