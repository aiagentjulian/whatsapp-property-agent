# Pearlmont — Facilities and Quality

## Purpose

Factual knowledge for Phase 1 facilities, green features, construction quality, clubhouse and common areas.

Primary source: `2026.09.21_PearlMont Phase 1_FAQ-update.pdf`.

Secondary source: `SkyWorld Pearlmont Project Brief - V4 FULL.pdf` where consistent with the newer FAQ.

## Level 9 facilities deck

- Main facilities level: Level 9
- Approximate facilities / landscape deck: 10 acres
- Podium landscape is not intended for public access
- Public access is limited to commercial areas and the pay-per-use clubhouse facilities
- fencing / separation is planned between the clubhouse facilities and the residents' podium landscape

## Community halls

- 2 community halls
- approximately 8,600 sq.ft. each
- estimated capacity: 250 people each
- available for booking on a first-come, first-served basis

## Parcel facilities

- Parcel lockers: 1,256 units
- Parcel / mailbox access: Level 4 drop-off / mailbox room

## Green building features

Pearlmont is stated to be GreenRE certified.

Project green features include:

- shallow and wide unit design to support natural ventilation and daylighting
- fully daylighted / ventilated development concept
- solar PV panels for compound/common-area lighting
- rainwater harvesting system
- EV charging bays

Project Brief design studies additionally state:

- 100% natural ventilation provision
- approximately 90% of unit area with daylighting

Treat those percentages as project-material design claims, not independent measurements.

## QLASSIC and construction quality

- Construction methodology: PPVC — Prefabricated Prefinished Volumetric Construction
- Minimum QLASSIC quality assurance target/standard: 85%
- FAQ states that if the minimum 85% QLASSIC score is not achieved, the defect liability period will be extended by 6 months upon VP
- Project materials state a 10-year leak-proof / waterproofing warranty, subject to terms and conditions

PPVC is described in the Project Brief as factory-manufactured 3D volumetric modules that are prefinished with internal finishes, fittings and M&E work before transport and assembly on site.

Do not convert marketing claims about quality or speed into guarantees beyond the official wording.

## Super Clubhouse

- Expected completion: together with Phase 1 residential, expected Q4 2029
- Approximate size: 50,000 sq.ft.
- Access: both residents and public
- Residents and registered tenants are stated to receive preferential / discounted rates compared with public users
- Managed by developer according to the FAQ

Main clubhouse facilities listed:

- Swimming pool
- Kids' pool / water-play area
- Fitness Centre
- Sports Complex
- Multipurpose Hall

Project Brief also illustrates badminton and pickleball within the Sports Complex concept.

## Pool details

FAQ states:

- Main swimming pool: approx. 55m length
- Main pool water depth: approx. 1.2m
- Wading pool: approx. 11m length
- Wading pool depth: approx. 0.35m
- Water treatment: chlorinated pool

Project Brief shows an adult infinity swimming pool/deck concept of approximately 8,000 sq.ft. and a kids water playland concept of approximately 2,000 sq.ft.

## Pay-per-use model

Selected clubhouse facilities use a pay-per-use model.

Project materials position the model as allowing users to pay based on usage rather than embedding all clubhouse costs into a fixed universal membership structure.

Do not quote example PPU prices from conceptual presentation slides as actual operating rates unless the latest operator pricing confirms them.

## Common-area infrastructure

- high-speed fibre optic: provided

## Facilities access caution

When discussing access rights, distinguish:

- residents' private podium landscape
- public/commercial areas
- pay-per-use clubhouse facilities that can be accessed by residents and public under their respective terms

Do not describe the entire facilities deck as public.
