# Pearlmont — School Access & Family

## Purpose
Use this file for factual questions about the vertical school, school access, resident privacy, family convenience, and related project facilities.

## Vertical School
Latest FAQ states:
- The school is planned as a 25-storey building.
- It is described as accommodating both SK and SMK.
- Operator / authority: Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia (MOE).
- It faces Plot 2.
- It is not directly in front of the residential towers and is stated not to block the residential view.
- School completion is expected together with Phase 1.

## School Access
- School can be accessed from Ground Floor and Level 9 podium.
- Residents can walk to the school through a covered walkway.
- School has its own designated parking area.

## Resident Privacy & Separation
- Non-resident students / parents are not intended to have unrestricted access to the residents' podium facilities.
- Access control is by access card / controlled access.
- Non-residents use the commercial carpark rather than the segregated residential carpark.

## Resident Registration Priority
- FAQ states priority would be given to residents, subject to MOE arrangement.
- This is not a guaranteed admission right.
- Do not promise school placement, enrollment approval, intake timing, catchment rights, or admission criteria unless confirmed by MOE / latest official material.

## Family-Oriented Facilities
Confirmed family/community components include:
- 2 Surau.
- Tadika.
- 2 Community Halls.
- Children's playground.
- 10-acre facilities / landscape deck.
- Swimming pool and kids' pool / water play facilities within the clubhouse concept.

## Sales Usage Rule
The school can be relevant for family buyers, but it must be presented as a project convenience / planned education component rather than a guaranteed school-admission benefit. Keep factual claims separate from later Buyer Fit / USP positioning.
