# Pearlmont — Green Building and Sustainability

## Purpose

Use this file when customers ask about GreenRE, ventilation, natural lighting, solar panels, rainwater harvesting, EV charging, energy efficiency, or other sustainability features.

This file contains project facts and project-material claims. Do not expand them into guaranteed utility savings, health outcomes, or investment returns.

---

## Green Certification

The project FAQ states that Pearlmont is GreenRE certified.

Project materials also position the development around low-carbon and healthy-home design principles.

---

## Natural Ventilation and Daylighting

The FAQ lists the following green-building features:

- shallow and wide unit design with natural ventilation and daylighting
- development design intended to improve daylight and ventilation

Project materials further state:

- 100% natural ventilation provision
- approximately 90% of unit area with daylighting

These are project-material design claims.

Do not convert them into guaranteed electricity-bill savings.

---

## Solar PV

Solar PV panels are stated to support compound / common-area lighting.

Use this as a common-area sustainability feature.

Do not tell customers that their individual unit receives direct solar electricity unless current technical documents explicitly say so.

---

## Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater harvesting is included among the project’s green features.

Project materials describe it as a common-area low-carbon / water-efficiency initiative.

Do not claim potable-water substitution unless specifically supported by current technical documentation.

---

## EV Charging

The FAQ states:

- total EV charging bays: 64
- 32 commercial
- 32 residential
- charging bays located at Level 1 and Level 8

The charging rate is stated as TBC in the FAQ.

Do not invent the operator, charging tariff, charging speed, or connector standard.

---

## Passive Design

Project materials highlight:

- daylight provision for habitable spaces
- natural ventilation for habitable spaces
- building-envelope design suited to the local climate
- reduced dependence on artificial lighting and air-conditioning as intended design benefits

These should be described as design intentions / project features, not guaranteed household outcomes.

---

## PPVC and Quality Link

The project uses Prefabricated Prefinished Volumetric Construction (PPVC).

Project materials position PPVC as supporting:

- more controlled factory production
- prefinished modules
- quality checking
- improved consistency of finishes

The project also carries a minimum QLASSIC assurance target of 85% according to the FAQ.

For detailed PPVC, quality, or warranty questions, retrieve `facilities-and-quality.md` and `specifications-and-finishes.md` as needed.

---

## Sustainability Positioning Boundary

Reasonable factual statements include:

- GreenRE certified
- solar PV for common-area / compound lighting
- rainwater harvesting
- EV charging bays
- natural ventilation and daylighting-oriented design

Avoid unsupported statements such as:

- “your electricity bill will definitely be lower by X%”
- “this guarantees healthier occupants”
- “GreenRE means the unit will appreciate faster”
- “the development is carbon neutral”

Those claims are not established by the supplied project materials.
