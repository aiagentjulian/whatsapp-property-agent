# Pearlmont — Maintenance & Management

## Purpose

This file contains factual maintenance, management, resident-access, and ongoing ownership information for SkyWorld Pearlmont Phase 1.

Use this file when the customer asks about maintenance fees, sinking fund, management, common-area operations, utility rooms, clubhouse management, resident privileges, or digital resident services.

Do not use this file as a sales script. Sales positioning belongs in the sales/USP layer.

---

## Maintenance Fee

- Maintenance fee: **RM0.18 per sq.ft.**
- The stated RM0.18 psf is **inclusive of sinking fund**.

For a standard 900 sq.ft. unit, do not automatically quote a monthly total unless the runtime is explicitly allowed to calculate it. If calculated, make clear that it is an estimate based on the stated rate and subject to the latest project confirmation.

---

## Management Structure

- The development is intended to have centralized property / facility management.
- The Super Clubhouse is stated to be managed by the developer.
- Swimming pool management is stated as AUM.
- Fitness Centre and Sports Complex operator is to be appointed by AUM upon completion.

Where the operator is described as future / to be appointed, do not present a specific operator as finalized.

---

## Super Clubhouse Access & Resident Privilege

- Super Clubhouse access is available to both residents and the public.
- Residents and registered tenants are stated to receive a special discounted rate compared with the public.
- Access is through designated lifts from ground floor, carpark floors, and the Level 9 facilities floor.
- Two community halls at Level 9 are available for booking on a first-come, first-served basis.
- The clubhouse does not list F&B or co-working space in the latest FAQ.

---

## Podium / Facilities Access

- The approximately 10-acre podium landscape is not intended for general public access.
- Public access is limited to the commercial area and pay-per-use clubhouse facilities.
- Fencing separates clubhouse/public-access areas from the residents' podium landscape.

Do not describe every facility as private to residents. The latest FAQ distinguishes between resident-only podium landscape and public-access pay-per-use clubhouse facilities.

---

## Utility Room Ownership & Charges

Where a unit has a utility room / accessory parcel:

- utility room passageway: minimum 900 mm
- approximate height: 2.7 m
- basic provision: 1 lighting point
- floor finish: cement screed
- maintenance charge: calculated based on **50% of the share unit** for the accessory parcel
- maintenance responsibility: unit owner
- extra utility rooms are not stated to be available for sale

## Resident Digital Platform

The project materials describe the **SkyWorld Connect App** as part of the resident ecosystem.

Functions referenced in project materials include:

- visitor management
- facility booking
- maintenance-fee payment integration
- tenant management
- community management
- parcel locker management
- e-forms and documents
- events and rewards
- construction progress updates before handover
- key collection appointment
- defect management

Treat feature availability and final implementation as subject to the developer's latest rollout.

## Access Cards / App

Latest FAQ states:

- resident access uses the SkyWorld Connect App
- one access card is provided
- designated-floor lift control forms part of the security arrangement

Do not imply unlimited cards or fully app-only access unless confirmed by the latest developer information.

## Parcel Management

- 1,256 parcel lockers are stated in the latest FAQ.
- Parcel / mailbox access is associated with the Level 4 drop-off area.
- Food, parcel, and delivery waiting is also stated at the Level 4 drop-off area.
