# Pearlmont — Risks & Sensitive FAQ

## Purpose

Use this file when the customer raises concerns that require careful, factual answers rather than marketing language.

These topics can materially affect trust. Answer directly, do not dismiss the concern, do not exaggerate safety, and do not invent reassurance beyond the project materials.

---

## High-Tension Cable / Pylon

Latest FAQ states for Phase 1 Block 1B:

- approximate distance from the high-tension cable: **120 ft / 36 m**
- minimum stated safe-distance requirement: **20 m**

Do not generalize this exact distance to every tower or every unit unless the source specifically supports it.

If the customer is highly concerned about electromagnetic exposure, legal compliance, or technical safety standards beyond these stated project facts, hand off for confirmation rather than making technical or medical claims.

---

## River / Flooding Concern

The development is beside a river / riverbank environment.

Latest FAQ states:

- river maintenance is under JPS / flood-gate freeboard control
- overall development finished floor level will be raised to approximately **2.4 m above sea level**
- original ground level is described as approximately 1.0–1.2 m
- this is stated as part of local-authority flood-level management compliance
- management office is expected to conduct fogging for Aedes prevention

Do not claim that flooding is impossible or that there is "zero flood risk."

Correct framing:

- acknowledge the concern
- explain the stated mitigation / design measures
- avoid absolute guarantees

---

## River Smell / Hygiene

Latest FAQ acknowledges the common concern that riverside developments may face hygiene or smell questions and states that the river is maintained by JPS.

The source does not guarantee that odor will never occur.

Do not invent water-quality data, smell-frequency claims, or environmental guarantees.

## Podium / Public Access

Not all facilities are resident-only.

The latest FAQ distinguishes:

- approximately 10-acre podium landscape: not public access
- commercial area: public access
- pay-per-use clubhouse: residents and public can access
- fencing separates public-access clubhouse areas from resident podium landscape

Do not market the entire clubhouse as residents-only private facilities.

---

## Vertical School & Privacy

Latest FAQ states:

- vertical school is planned as a 25-storey building
- school categories: SK and SMK
- operator / owner: Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia
- school is stated not to directly block the residential tower view
- connection from ground floor and Level 9 podium
- residents can walk via covered walkway
- non-resident students / parents are controlled from resident podium facilities through access control
- non-residents are stated to use the segregated commercial carpark rather than residential carpark
- residents may receive priority for school registration, subject to MOE arrangement

Do not guarantee school admission. Any priority is explicitly subject to MOE arrangement.

## PPVC Modification Limits

Because the project uses PPVC construction, modification questions must be answered carefully.

Latest FAQ states:

- concealed power sockets cannot simply be added by purchaser
- air-con drainpipes are provided to bedrooms and living hall
- copper piping is provided to living hall and master bedroom under the upgrading package
- pipe sleeves are provided in bedrooms 2 and 3 for future owner installation

The broader project material discusses hackable vs non-hackable walls, but final renovation must follow the Home Owner Manual and developer / management rules.

Do not tell buyers they may hack or modify structural / service walls freely.

---

## Water Leakage / Warranty

Project materials state a **10-year waterproofing / leak-proof warranty**, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

Latest FAQ states that after the 10-year waterproofing warranty expires, maintenance and repair responsibilities are governed by the applicable Strata Management Act 2013 provisions.

Do not describe this as a blanket 10-year warranty covering every defect in the property.

## QLASSIC

Latest FAQ states:

- minimum QLASSIC assurance target: **85%**
- if the stated target is not achieved, defect liability period is extended another 6 months upon VP

Do not say Pearlmont has already achieved a final 85% score before the project is completed and assessed.

## Completion Timing

Latest FAQ states Phase 1 expected completion: **Q4 2029**.

Older project material includes earlier tentative dates such as September 2029.

Use the newer FAQ date when answering current customer questions.

## General Sensitive-Question Rule

For concerns involving:

- legal interpretation
- loan eligibility
- technical safety
- environmental risk
- exact unit availability
- exact current package
- renovation permission
- school admission
- developer contractual commitments

answer only what the current source supports.

If the customer's decision depends on a fact that is not confirmed, say it needs confirmation and move toward human handoff when appropriate.
