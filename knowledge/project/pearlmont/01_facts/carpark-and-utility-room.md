# Pearlmont — Carpark & Utility Room

## Purpose
Use this file for factual questions about carpark allocation, parking levels, bay type, EV charging, extra parking, utility rooms, and related maintenance.

## Carpark Podium
- Carpark podium spans Ground Floor to Level 8.
- Residential carpark: Level 2 to Level 8.
- Visitor carpark: Level 8.
- Motorcycle parking: Ground Floor to Level 8.

## Parking Bay Size
- Perpendicular parking: approximately 2.4 m × 4.8 m.
- Side parking: approximately 2.5 m × 6.0 m.
- Parking is stated as side-by-side rather than tandem.

## Parking Allocation Per Unit
- Typical 900 sq ft 3R2B unit: 1 or 2 carparks depending on selected unit/category.
- Carpark allocation varies by tower, floor, facing/category, and sales package.
- Do not infer a specific unit's carpark count from general project information. Use the relevant pricing / sales-package knowledge for unit-specific confirmation.

## Extra Parking
- Extra carpark bays are not stated as available for sale.
- Extra carpark bays may be available for rental.
- Availability is not guaranteed and should be confirmed when relevant.

## EV Charging
- 64 EV charging bays are stated in the latest FAQ.
- EV charging locations include Level 1 and Level 8.
- Charging rate is TBC in the FAQ.

## Carpark Access
- Express ramp sections connect Ground Floor to Level 4 and Level 4 to Level 8.
- Main entrance has separate lanes for residents, visitors, and motorcycles.
- Residential and commercial parking/access are segregated.

## Utility Rooms
- Latest FAQ states 490 utility rooms across the development context referenced in the document.
- Passageway to utility room: minimum approximately 900 mm.
- Utility room height: approximately 2.7 m.
- Typical utility room provision: 1 lighting point.
- Flooring: cement screed.
- Wheel stopper near utility room: not provided.
- Utility-room maintenance charge is calculated based on 50% of the share unit as an accessory parcel.

- Extra utility rooms are not stated as available for sale.

## Sales Usage Rule
For normal customer conversations, only surface the parking details that matter to the buyer: 1 vs 2 carparks, side-by-side allocation, visitor parking, EV charging, or extra-rental possibility. Do not overwhelm customers with total-bay counts unless they ask.
