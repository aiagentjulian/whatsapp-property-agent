# Pearlmont — Vertical School and Commercial Component

## Purpose

Factual knowledge for the planned vertical school and commercial component within the Pearlmont development.

Primary source: `2026.09.21_PearlMont Phase 1_FAQ-update.pdf`.

## Vertical school

### Building and operator

- Planned height: 25 storeys
- Operator / authority: Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia (MOE)
- School categories stated in FAQ: SK and SMK

## Position and view

The FAQ states the school faces Plot 2 but is not directly in front of the residential towers and is not expected to block the residential view.

Do not convert this into a blanket guarantee for every unit; exact unit views depend on tower, level and orientation.

## Connectivity

- direct access from Ground Floor and Level 9 podium
- covered walkway for residents walking to school
- designated school parking compound

## Access control

- non-resident students/parents are not intended to access the residents' podium facilities area freely
- access is controlled by access card
- non-resident users are directed to commercial/segregated parking rather than residential parking

## Resident enrolment priority

FAQ states residents are expected to receive priority for school registration, subject to MOE arrangement.

This is not an unconditional admission guarantee.

## Completion

- school completion: stated to be together with Phase 1

Current Phase 1 completion reference: expected Q4 2029.

## Commercial component

FAQ states:

- retail shops are for sale
- retail area serves residents and public
- commercial built-up area: approximately 321,418 sq.ft.
- number of commercial units: 146
- dedicated commercial visitor/staff parking is available at Ground Floor
- no centralised air-conditioning system
- expected commercial completion: together with Phase 1

## Sales-use caution

The school and commercial component can be relevant to family-living and convenience discussions, but do not present future tenant mix, retail brands, student enrolment certainty or commercial performance unless there is a separate verified source.
