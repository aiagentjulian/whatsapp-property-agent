# Pearlmont — Parking, Access and Security

## Purpose

Factual knowledge for parking allocation, access routes, EV charging, security layers and resident movement.

Primary source: `2026.09.21_PearlMont Phase 1_FAQ-update.pdf`.

Secondary source: `SkyWorld Pearlmont Project Brief - V4 FULL.pdf` for access-system descriptions where consistent with the FAQ.

## Parking structure

- Car-park podium: Ground Floor to Level 8
- Commercial parking: Ground Floor and Level 1
- Residential parking: Level 2 to Level 8
- Visitor parking: Level 8
- Motorcycle parking: Ground Floor to Level 8

## Parking allocation per residential unit

- Typical 900 sq.ft. unit: 1 or 2 single car parks depending on unit/category
- Parking lots are stated as side-by-side, not tandem
- Extra car parks are not sold separately
- Extra car parks may be available for rental according to the FAQ

For a specific unit's allocation, use the current unit/package data rather than assuming from block alone.

## Car-park bay sizes

- Perpendicular parking: approximately 2.4m x 4.8m
- Side parking: approximately 2.5m x 6.0m

## Parking totals in the master development

FAQ lists:

- Commercial car parks: 1,475
- Resident car parks: 9,576
- Visitor car parks: 574
- OKU car parks: 5 commercial + 8 residential
- Motorcycles: 943 commercial + 2,897 residential

These totals are broader development/car-park figures and should not be confused with Phase 1 unit allocation counts.

## EV charging

- Total EV charging bays: 64
- 32 commercial
- 32 residential
- EV charging locations: Level 1 and Level 8
- Charging rate: TBC in the FAQ

Do not invent charging fees or operator details.

## Vehicle circulation

- Car-park ramp clear width: approximately 3.8m and 4.075m
- Speed ramps / express movement: Ground to Level 4 and Level 4 to Level 8
- Main entrance ingress: 6,000mm for 2 car lanes + 900mm motorcycle lane
- Main entrance egress: 6,000mm for 2 car lanes + 900mm motorcycle lane

## Security layers

FAQ states 4 tiers of security:

1. Guard House
2. Security Patrolling
3. Lift Lobby
4. Designated-floor Lift Control

## Resident access

- Main resident access ecosystem: SkyWorld Connect App
- 1 access card is stated as provided
- designated-floor lift access is part of the security concept

Project Brief describes SkyWorld Connect App functions such as:

- visitor management
- facility booking
- maintenance-fee payment integration
- tenant management
- parcel-locker management
- defect management
- key-collection appointments
- communication with management/developer

Treat app feature availability/timing as project-system functionality and confirm if a customer asks about a specific feature at handover.

## CCTV

FAQ states CCTV coverage includes:

- car-park ramps
- lift cars
- guard house
- mailbox and parcel room
- major common areas

## Entrance lanes

Three access ways are stated:

- Residents Lane
- Visitors Lane
- Motorcycle Lane

## Residential drop-off and link bridges

- Main residential drop-off: Level 4
- Link bridges connect the podium to all residential towers
- Blocks 1B/1C: Levels 2, 4, 6, 8, 9
- Block 1D: Levels 3, 4, 6, 8, 9

## Commercial / residential segregation

Residential and commercial car-park circulation is separated.

Non-resident school users are intended to access commercial/designated parking rather than resident parking.

This segregation can be explained when relevant to privacy/security concerns, but should not be exaggerated into claims that unauthorized access is impossible.
