# Pearlmont — Building & Access

## Purpose
Use this file for factual questions about building levels, lifts, corridors, drop-off, link bridges, access flow, and residential/common-area circulation.

## Phase 1 Building Structure
- Phase 1 has 3 residential towers: Block 1B, Block 1C, and Block 1D.
- Block 1B: 38 levels.
- Block 1C: 38 levels.
- Block 1D: 37 levels.
- Units per floor:
  - Block 1B: 15 units.
  - Block 1C: 15 units.
  - Block 1D: 18 units.
- Each block has 4 passenger lifts plus 1 BOMBA lift.
- Passenger lift capacity: 19 persons.
- BOMBA lift capacity: 19 persons.

## Podium & Access Levels
- Ground Floor and Level 1: commercial component / commercial carpark.
- Level 2 to Level 8: residential carpark.
- Level 9: residential Skypark and pay-per-use clubhouse facilities.

## Residential Drop-off & Link Bridges
- Residential drop-off is at Level 4 carpark.
- Link bridge access connects the podium to each tower.
- Link bridge levels:
  - Blocks 1B and 1C: Levels 2, 4, 6, 8, and 9.
  - Block 1D: Levels 3, 4, 6, 8, and 9.
- Project Brief also describes recurring link-bridge connectivity for Blocks 1B and 1C at selected higher levels. For customer-facing replies, use only the access detail required by the question and avoid overloading with every connection level unless specifically asked.

## Express Ramps
- Two express-ramp sections are described:
  - Ground to Level 4, branching to lower residential carpark levels.
  - Level 4 to Level 8, branching to upper residential carpark levels.

## Typical Building Dimensions
- Typical unit-floor corridor clear width: approximately 1.65 m.
- Common-area staircase width: approximately 1.1 m.
- Emergency staircase width: approximately 1.1 m.
- Typical unit-floor corridor floor-to-floor height: approximately 2,950 mm.
- Typical corridor floor-to-ceiling height: approximately 2,400 mm.

## Water / Service Infrastructure
- Domestic water tank locations: ground-floor podium area and tower rooftop.
- No intermediate break tank is stated in the latest FAQ.
- Master refuse chamber and bulk waste room: Ground Floor.

## Delivery & Visitor Movement
- Dedicated parcel / mail / food-delivery waiting area: Level 4 drop-off.
- Visitor carpark: Level 8.
- Separate entrance/access arrangements exist for residential and commercial areas.

## Sales Usage Rule
This is factual reference material. Do not volunteer detailed circulation or engineering information unless it helps answer a customer concern. For normal sales conversations, summarize the practical benefit first, such as residential/commercial separation, dedicated drop-off, and direct access to facilities.
