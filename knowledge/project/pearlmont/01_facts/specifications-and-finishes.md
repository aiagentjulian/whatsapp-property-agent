# Pearlmont — Specifications and Finishes

## Purpose

Use this file for customer questions about unit finishes, flooring, walls, doors, ceiling, electrical points, air-conditioning provisions, sanitary fittings, balcony details, windows, and internal specifications.

Use factual wording only. Do not imply that items are provided when the source only states that a point, sleeve, pipe, or provision is included.

---

- 900 sq.ft.
- 3 bedrooms
- 2 bathrooms
- balcony

## Electrical and M&E Points

Base provision stated in the FAQ includes:

- 12 lighting points
- 2 fan points
- 1 SMATV point
- 11 x 13A power points
- 2 air-conditioning points
- 2 water-heater points
- 1 fibre wall socket

The upgrade package referenced in project materials includes additional provisions such as:

- additional fan hooks / fan points for bedroom 2 and bedroom 3
- upgraded two-gang switches for bedroom 2 and bedroom 3
- concealed / prepared air-conditioning copper piping with ceiling box for living area and master bedroom

Do not simplify this into “all rooms come with air-conditioning piping”.

The FAQ states:

- drainpipe is provided to all bedrooms and living hall;
- copper pipe is provided to living hall and master bedroom under the upgrading package;
- pipe sleeves are provided for bedroom 2 and bedroom 3 for later installation.

## PPVC Modification Caution

The project uses PPVC construction.

The source materials distinguish hackable and non-hackable walls and instruct owners to refer to the Home Owner Manual for embedded services.

Do not advise a customer to hack, drill, conceal wiring, or modify structural / PPVC elements without reference to the approved owner documentation and professional advice.

The FAQ specifically states that concealed power sockets cannot simply be added.
