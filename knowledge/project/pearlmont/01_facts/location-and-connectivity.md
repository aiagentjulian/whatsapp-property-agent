# Pearlmont — Location and Connectivity

## Purpose

Factual knowledge for where Pearlmont is, how the site connects internally and externally, and what location-related claims are supported by the provided project materials.

Primary source: `2026.09.21_PearlMont Phase 1_FAQ-update.pdf`.

Secondary source: `SkyWorld Pearlmont Project Brief - V4 FULL.pdf` for masterplan/connectivity descriptions.

## Exact location

- Mukim 1, Seberang Jaya, Seberang Perai Tengah, Pulau Pinang, 13700
- Project materials position the development in Seberang Jaya / Penang Mainland

The Agent may know the exact location internally, but customer-facing disclosure is controlled by sales strategy. Do not automatically reveal the full project identity or exact location at the very start of every conversation.

## External connectivity

Project materials describe connectivity toward:

- Sunway Carnival Mall
- Chai Leng Park
- Butterworth / mainland urban network

The Project Brief also shows Butterworth Jetty and surrounding roads on location maps, but exact driving times/distances should not be invented unless a current verified source is available.

## Public transport

The FAQ states public transportation is easily accessible from the development.

Do not invent a specific station, frequency, walking time or route unless supported by a specific current source.

## Road and neighbourhood design

The Project Brief describes a 66-foot road in front of the development and a community-centric mobility plan featuring:

- dedicated walkways
- bicycle paths
- non-motorized scooter provision
- landscaped edges
- pedestrian/cyclist lanes

These are project/masterplan descriptions and should be phrased as planned/provided features where appropriate.

## Riverside connection

- Approximate distance from the development to the riverside jogging park/lane: 10m
- Project materials position riverside living / river promenade as part of the development concept

## Flood and river-related information

The FAQ states:

- finished floor level of the overall development will be raised to 2.4m above sea level
- original ground level is stated as approximately 1m–1.2m
- this is described as compliance with local-authority flood-level management
- management office is expected to conduct fogging for Aedes prevention
- the river is maintained by JPS, with flood-gate/freeboard control referenced

Do not expand this into a guarantee that flooding can never occur.

## Internal development access

Residential and commercial access are segregated.

- Commercial car park: Ground Floor and Level 1
- Residential car park: Level 2 to Level 8
- Visitor car park: Level 8
- Level 9: residential Skypark and pay-per-use clubhouse

## Residential drop-off

- Residential drop-off: Level 4 car park
- Link bridge from podium to each residential tower

Link-bridge floors from the FAQ / Project Brief:

- Blocks 1B and 1C: Levels 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9
- Block 1D: Levels 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9

The FAQ also describes additional connectivity between Blocks 1B and 1C at intervals beginning from Level 4.

## Express ramps

Project materials describe two express-ramp sets:

- Ground to Level 4, branching to lower residential parking levels
- Level 4 to Level 8, branching to upper residential parking levels

This is a useful functional point when discussing parking convenience, but exact driving experience should not be overstated before completion.

## Nearby school connection

The vertical school is planned to connect directly to the development from Ground Floor and Level 9 podium.

- covered walkway for residents walking to school
- designated school parking
- non-resident school users are intended to use segregated commercial parking rather than residential parking

See `vertical-school-and-commercial.md` for full school details.

## Sales-use caution

Location benefits can be used selectively based on customer need, but do not reveal every detail at once. Do not manufacture commute times, distances, rental catchment claims or future infrastructure that are not in the source material.
