# Pearlmont — Clubhouse and Pay-Per-Use

## Purpose

Use this file when customers ask about the Super Clubhouse, swimming pool, gym / fitness, sports facilities, community halls, who can access the clubhouse, whether facilities are free, or how the pay-per-use model works.

This file separates the private residential podium facilities from the public / pay-per-use clubhouse concept.

---

## Level 9 Facilities

The project FAQ states that the main facilities are located at Level 9.

The project includes approximately 10 acres of facilities / landscape deck.

The podium landscape itself is not open to the general public.

Public access is limited to commercial areas and the pay-per-use clubhouse facilities, with separation between public clubhouse access and the residential podium landscape.

## Pay-Per-Use Model

The clubhouse uses a pay-per-use concept for selected facilities.

The concept is intended to separate clubhouse usage cost from the normal residential maintenance fee so residents do not necessarily pay the same fixed clubhouse cost regardless of actual usage.

Project materials describe flexible use options such as:

- ad-hoc / pay-per-visit access
- frequent-user plans
- longer-duration packages

Do not quote example PPU prices from conceptual slides as final operating prices unless they are explicitly confirmed as current official rates.

---

## Resident vs Public Access

The FAQ states that both residents and the public may access the Super Clubhouse.

Residents and registered tenants are expected to receive a special discounted rate compared with the public.

The residential podium landscape remains separated from public areas.

This distinction is important when responding to privacy questions.

## Maintenance Fee Relationship

Residential maintenance fee is separately stated as RM0.18 psf inclusive of sinking fund.

Do not imply that every clubhouse activity is included in the RM0.18 psf fee.

The pay-per-use clubhouse concept exists precisely because selected clubhouse facilities are charged based on use / package rather than being treated as a universal flat-use benefit.

---

## Agent Guidance

If the customer says:

- “Why pay for facilities?”
- “Is the gym free?”
- “Can outsiders use the pool?”
- “Is the Sky Park public?”

Answer the exact question first and preserve the distinction between:

1. private / resident podium landscape and residential facilities;
2. selected pay-per-use clubhouse facilities;
3. public / commercial access zones.

Do not oversell the PPU model before understanding whether the customer sees it as a benefit or a concern.
