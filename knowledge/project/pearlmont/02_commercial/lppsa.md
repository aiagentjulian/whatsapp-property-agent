# Pearlmont — LPPSA Financing

## Purpose

Use this file when a customer asks about LPPSA / government-sector housing financing.

LPPSA eligibility is regulated and customer-specific. The Agent may explain the current promotional information supplied to the sales team, but must not guarantee eligibility, approval, financing margin, or rate for a specific customer.

---

## Current Reference

The supplied LPPSA update is marked:

**Effective 4 October 2026**

Key points stated in the supplied material:

1. Maximum financing eligibility up to **RM1,000,000**.
2. A second LPPSA financing is allowed without fully settling the first financing.
3. Up to **90% financing margin** if the first financing is still active.
4. **4.00%** rate for financing up to **RM750,000**.
5. **4.50%** applies only to the portion above **RM750,000**.

The material explicitly states that eligibility is subject to **LPPSA terms and conditions**.

---

## Agent Behaviour

Allowed:

- explain that LPPSA may be relevant for eligible government-sector buyers
- explain the stated financing limits / margin / rates from the current reference
- ask whether the customer is considering LPPSA if it naturally helps the conversation
- offer to have the latest eligibility / financing details confirmed

Not allowed:

- "You definitely qualify"
- "You can definitely get 90%"
- "Your rate will definitely be 4.00%"
- making legal or eligibility determinations from chat alone

---

## Sales Relevance

LPPSA can be commercially relevant when the customer is a government-sector buyer or mentions:

- civil service / government employment
- LPPSA
- second-home financing
- concern over loan margin
- monthly commitment

Do not force LPPSA into a conversation where it is not relevant.

---

## Freshness Rule

This is time-sensitive financing information.

If a newer LPPSA update is added later, replace or supersede this file rather than combining old and new rates.

When there is uncertainty, confirm the latest terms before using the information to support a booking decision.
