# Pearlmont — Financing and Booking

## Purpose

Factual knowledge for panel financiers, loan margins, booking refund rules, progressive payment, Bumi-related purchase terms and booking-stage cautions.

Primary source: `2026.09.21_PearlMont Phase 1_FAQ-update.pdf`.

LPPSA is maintained separately because it is a specific government-financing route and can change independently.

## Loan-margin references in the FAQ

The FAQ lists the following as examples of end-financier support:

### Up to 95%

- PBB
- HLB / HLBB
- RHB — GreenRE
- MBB — GreenRE

### Up to 100%

- PBB
- Bank Islam
- MBB under SJKP
- RHB
- HLBB

These are source-listed possibilities, not a guarantee of customer approval.

The Agent must not tell a customer that they definitely qualify for a specific margin based only on conversation.

Actual financing depends on lender assessment, eligibility, documentation, income/commitment profile and current programme terms.

## Booking-fee refund rule

FAQ states:

- booking fee is fully refundable with 2 loan-rejection letters
- otherwise, RM300 is forfeited if the purchaser cancels without loan rejection

Do not expand this into legal advice. If the customer is close to booking, exact current booking/refund terms should be reconfirmed with the human salesperson / latest booking form.

## Progressive payment

FAQ states:

- approved progressive-payment schedule follows KPKT approval

If purchaser uses 100% loan margin:

- first disbursement of the 10% sum to developer is upon confirmation and advice from the solicitor

Do not invent the stage-by-stage KPKT schedule unless the current approved schedule is actually available.

## Land encumbrance

FAQ states land encumbrance: HLBB.

If a customer asks for legal interpretation of encumbrance, title release, redemption or conveyancing implications, do not improvise. Handoff / confirm with the appropriate human or solicitor.

## Bumi terms

FAQ states:

- Bumi discount: 5%
- Bumi quota: 30%
- Bumi discount is reflected in the SPA
- purchaser who bought under Bumi discount may sell to non-Bumi after 10 years according to the FAQ

Treat the 10-year resale point as source-derived project guidance, not broad legal advice.

## Purchaser unit limit

FAQ states:

- maximum of 2 units per purchaser, including Batu Kawan

If customer circumstances are unusual or involve multiple names/entities, confirm current developer policy rather than extrapolating.

## Booking/payment process

Payment destination / developer DuitNow QR information is operational booking information and should not be exposed as a general FAQ answer.

The Agent should not proactively send payment instructions early in the sales conversation.

Once the lead reaches the booking/payment stage, ownership will normally already be with a human salesperson. The latest payment destination must be verified before the customer is instructed to transfer money.

## Sales-stage rule

Financing information should support conversion toward viewing/appointment, not turn the Agent into a mortgage adviser.

Useful early-stage behaviour includes:

- clarify whether customer expects loan or cash purchase when relevant
- explain that high-margin options may exist
- avoid making approval promises
- use financing concerns to identify what needs human/banker follow-up

## Accuracy rule

Any statement involving:

- loan eligibility
- approval probability
- monthly instalment
- effective interest/profit rate
- legal ownership restrictions
- payment destination

must use current verified inputs. Do not guess.
