# Pearlmont — Panel Financiers and Legal

## Purpose

Use this file when a customer asks about panel banks, legal firms, loan support, booking refund conditions, land encumbrance, or transaction/legal support.

This file is factual project knowledge. It is not legal or financing advice.

---

## Panel End Financiers

The FAQ lists the following panel end financiers for mortgage / loan services:

- Maybank (MBB)
- RHB
- Public Bank (PBB)
- CIMB
- Hong Leong Bank (HLBB / HLB)
- Bank Islam
- UOB
- Affin Bank
- Alliance Bank

Do not imply that being a panel financier guarantees approval.

Loan approval, margin, rate, eligibility, and final terms remain subject to the respective bank and current programme conditions.

---

## Financing Margin References

The current FAQ states that some end financiers may support higher financing margins under qualifying programmes:

### Up to 95%

- Public Bank
- Hong Leong Bank
- RHB under Green RE
- Maybank under Green RE

### Up to 100%

The FAQ lists the following examples:

- Public Bank
- Bank Islam
- Maybank under SJKP
- RHB
- Hong Leong Bank

These are programme references, not automatic customer entitlements.

Do not tell a customer they will receive 95% or 100% financing without an actual assessment.

## Booking Refund Reference

The FAQ states that the booking fee is fully refundable with two loan rejection letters.

If cancellation happens without the required loan rejection support, the FAQ states that RM300 will be forfeited.

Use the latest booking policy before confirming any refund outcome.

---

## 100% Financing and First Disbursement

For purchasers applying for 100% financing, the FAQ states that the first 10% disbursement to the developer is subject to confirmation and advice from the solicitor.

Do not invent a fixed disbursement date.

---

## Progressive Payment

The approved progressive payment schedule follows KPKT approval according to the project FAQ.

If a customer needs the exact stage-by-stage payment schedule, use the current approved transaction documents or hand off to a human salesperson / solicitor.

---

## Agent Response Rules

When customers ask financing or legal questions:

1. answer the factual project question first;
2. distinguish panel availability from approval certainty;
3. do not guarantee financing margin, eligibility, interest rate, or legal outcome;
4. do not provide legal interpretation beyond the supplied project documents;
5. when the customer is serious and financing becomes a real purchase blocker, move toward assessment / human follow-up rather than over-explaining on WhatsApp.
