## 1. Affordable Entry + 900 sqft + 3R2B + Freehold

### Core proposition
A 900 sq.ft., three-bedroom, two-bathroom, freehold home with a balcony. Base pricing and a dated rebate package are documented separately; check the current package and exact unit pricing before quoting a live offer.

## 2. Mature Seberang Jaya Location

### Core proposition
The project is in an established Penang Mainland location with connectivity toward Sunway Carnival Mall, Chai Leng Park and Butterworth. Do not invent journey times.

## 3. 10-Acre Elevated SkyPark / Large Community Green Space

### Core proposition
Project materials describe an approximately 10-acre Level 9 landscape and community deck. Distinguish residents' podium landscape from the separate pay-per-use clubhouse.

## 4. Vertical School + Family Ecosystem

### Core proposition
A vertical school and covered connection are planned as part of the development. School admission and operation are not guaranteed; resident priority is subject to MOE arrangements.

## 5. PPVC + Quality Assurance + 10-Year Waterproofing Warranty

### Core proposition
The materials describe PPVC construction, an 85% minimum QLASSIC quality target, a conditional DLP extension, and a 10-year waterproofing commitment subject to terms. These are not claims that the unfinished project has already achieved a final score.

## 6. RM0.18 psf Maintenance + Pay-Per-Use Clubhouse

### Core proposition
The referenced maintenance fee is RM0.18 per sq.ft. inclusive of sinking fund; selected clubhouse facilities have separate pay-per-use charges. Confirm current contractual rates and terms when needed.

## 7. Integrated Community + Commercial Component

### Core proposition
The masterplan combines residential towers with planned commercial and community spaces, with distinct access and parking arrangements. Specific future tenants are not confirmed.

## 8. Green / Healthy Home Design

### Core proposition
GreenRE certification and design provisions for daylight, ventilation, solar PV, rainwater harvesting and EV charging are referenced in project materials. Do not promise individual utility savings.

## 9. Efficient 900 sqft Layout

### Core proposition
The 900 sq.ft. Type A plan includes three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a balcony, an open living/dining/kitchen area and relatively limited internal corridors. Approximate room areas are documented in the unit-and-layout facts; furniture fit requires plan confirmation.

## 10. Freehold

### Core proposition
The project has freehold tenure. It does not establish guaranteed future price appreciation.
