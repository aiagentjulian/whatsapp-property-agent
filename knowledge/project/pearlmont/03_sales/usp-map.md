# Pearlmont USP Map

## Purpose

This file converts project facts into usable sales angles for the WhatsApp Sales Agent.

The Agent is a salesperson first. Its job is not to dump every project fact. It should identify what matters to the customer, use the most relevant USP, build buying interest, and move the conversation toward a confirmed viewing / appointment.

Do not treat this file as a script. Use it as a relevance map.

---

## 1. Affordable Entry + 900 sqft + 3R2B + Freehold

### Core proposition
A practical family-size home at an accessible entry point, without giving up freehold tenure or a full 3-bedroom layout.

### Why this matters
Many affordability-focused projects compete mainly on price. Pearlmont can be positioned around the combination of:
- current package / rebate-adjusted entry price
- 900 sqft
- 3 bedrooms
- 2 bathrooms
- balcony
- freehold tenure

The combination is more important than any one feature alone.

### Best-fit buyers
- first-time home buyers
- young couples planning for children
- small families
- buyers upgrading from renting
- buyers who want a usable family layout but are price-sensitive

### Best moment to use
- customer asks price first
- customer says budget is limited
- customer compares Pearlmont with smaller units
- customer says they need 3 rooms
- customer worries that affordable housing will feel cramped

### Sales use
Lead with the rebate-adjusted current entry price when appropriate, then connect the price to the 900 sqft / 3R2B / freehold combination.

Do not expose the full pricing matrix unless useful.

### Caution
Do not quote a unit-specific final price unless the applicable block, floor, facing, carpark allocation and current package are confirmed.

## 2. Mature Seberang Jaya Location

### Core proposition
Pearlmont is positioned inside an established Penang Mainland urban area rather than depending entirely on future township development.

### Why this matters
For own-stay buyers, mature surroundings reduce the uncertainty of waiting years for daily conveniences, healthcare, shopping and road connectivity to appear.

### Best-fit buyers
- own-stay families
- buyers already living or working around Seberang Perai
- buyers who prioritise convenience
- buyers with parents or children
- buyers who dislike isolated new townships

### Supporting themes
- established Seberang Jaya / Penang Mainland catchment
- nearby shopping and healthcare
- road connectivity
- access toward Penang Bridge / Butterworth side
- surrounding existing neighbourhoods

### Best moment to use
- customer asks where the project is
- customer says location is more important than facilities
- customer is comparing a new township with an established area
- customer has family/work commitments in mainland Penang

### Reveal strategy
The exact project identity and exact location should not automatically be disclosed at the beginning of every conversation.

Use the location benefit first when appropriate. Reveal full identity/location when the conversation and buyer intent justify it, especially closer to viewing.

## 5. PPVC + Quality Assurance + 10-Year Waterproofing Warranty

### Core proposition
A construction-quality story based on a more controlled building method plus defined quality and waterproofing commitments.

### Why this matters
Defects, leakage and workmanship are common buyer concerns in new high-rise properties. This USP directly addresses those fears.

### Best-fit buyers
- quality-sensitive buyers
- buyers who have had bad defect experiences
- buyers sceptical about affordable projects
- buyers worried about leakage
- buyers comparing developer build quality

### Supporting facts
- PPVC construction method
- minimum 85% QLASSIC quality assurance stated in project materials
- defect liability extension mechanism if the stated QLASSIC threshold is not achieved
- 10-year waterproofing / leak-proof warranty referenced in project materials

### Best moment to use
- customer asks about build quality
- customer says low price may mean lower quality
- customer raises defect or leakage concerns
- customer compares with a conventional construction project

### Caution
Do not describe the project as defect-free or leak-proof forever.

Use the actual stated quality commitments and warranty terms only.

## 9. Efficient 900 sqft Layout

### Core proposition
The 900 sqft is intended to be usable rather than heavily consumed by internal corridors or wasted circulation.

### Why this matters
Two homes with the same built-up can feel very different. Layout efficiency can make a 900 sqft unit feel more practical for real family use.

### Best-fit buyers
- young families
- first-time buyers
- work-from-home buyers
- buyers comparing unit size
- buyers who care about practical furniture placement

### Supporting themes from project materials
- 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms
- all bedrooms intended to accommodate queen-size beds
- natural light / outlook from habitable rooms
- efficient layout with limited wasted corridor area
- entrance foyer adds privacy
- balcony

### Best moment to use
- customer says 900 sqft sounds small
- customer compares with another 900–1,000 sqft project
- customer asks if bedrooms are usable
- customer asks whether the unit works for a family

### Caution
Furniture fit and perceived spaciousness should be demonstrated at the show unit where possible.

This USP naturally supports a viewing close: "Better to see the actual layout because the usable space is easier to judge in person."

# Sales Priority Guidance

The Agent should not list all USPs.

A useful default ranking for initial sales relevance is:

1. Current effective entry price + 900 sqft + 3R2B + freehold
2. Mature Seberang Jaya location
3. 10-acre SkyPark / family lifestyle
4. Vertical school / family convenience
5. PPVC + QLASSIC + waterproofing warranty
6. RM0.18 psf maintenance + PPU clubhouse
7. Efficient layout
8. Integrated commercial / community component
9. Green / healthy-home design

This ranking is not fixed. Customer relevance overrides the default order.

# Relevance Rules

## Do
- identify what the customer cares about first
- use one or two relevant USPs at a time
- connect features to customer needs
- answer the customer's immediate question before selling further
- move naturally toward a viewing when the project appears relevant
- use viewing as the main conversion objective

## Do not
- dump the entire project brochure into chat
- reveal every project detail too early
- force unrelated USPs into the conversation
- treat every buyer the same
- exaggerate facts
- guarantee financing, returns, school placement, future tenants, appreciation or availability

---

# Conversion Principle

The purpose of a USP is not merely to inform.

A good USP should help the Agent do one of four things:

1. create interest
2. increase perceived fit
3. reduce a meaningful objection
4. move the customer closer to a confirmed viewing / appointment

If a USP does none of these for the current customer, it probably does not need to be used yet.
